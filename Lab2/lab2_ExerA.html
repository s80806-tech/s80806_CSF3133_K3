About the Event

Faculty Programming Day is a one-day event organised for students who are interested in programming and technology. Students can learn new skills, share ideas and explore future career opportunities.

Speaker 1: Introduction to Web Development

Mr. Ahmad is a web developer who will introduce students to HTML and explain how websites are created. Students will learn the basics of building a webpage.

Speaker 2: Career Opportunities in IT

Ms. Sarah is an IT professional who will share her experience in the technology industry. She will discuss career opportunities and the skills needed by future graduates.
